Our Mission

PaperMentorAI was created to give students the opportunity to work directly with experienced AI researchers on meaningful, project-based research. We help students develop strong technical foundations, discover their academic interests, and build high-impact research portfolios that support university and future STEM goals.

Our Mentors

All mentors at PaperMentorAI are graduate students in Computer Science, AI, or related fields from top-tier international universities. They bring a high level of academic excellence, research depth, and hands-on experience with modern AI technologies.

In addition to their academic backgrounds, our mentors also have real-world industry experience at leading technology and AI companies. They work with cutting-edge machine learning systems daily and integrate these skills into their teaching and project guidance.

Most importantly, our mentors have years of experience guiding students through research projects, competitions, publications, and university preparation. Through PaperMentorAI, they have helped students gain admission to top universities and highly competitive master's programs, providing both academic and long-term career support.

Program Leadership

PaperMentorAI is led by an experienced AI researchers with a background in advanced machine learning and interdisciplinary research. With years of teaching and mentorship experience, the program lead ensures that every student receives high-quality, structured, and personalized research guidance.
