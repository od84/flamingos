Mission & Philosophy

Our goal is to bridge the gap between classroom learning and genuine research. Instead of simply following tutorials or completing a standard coding project, students learn how to define a research question, work with real data, design experiments, evaluate results, and communicate their findings clearly.

  • Understand how modern machine-learning methods work in practice.
  • Develop a focused research question around a real problem.
  • Implement and evaluate models using real-world datasets.
  • Compare approaches, interpret results, and identify limitations.
  • Develop a structured research paper or technical report.
  • Explore appropriate publication, competition, or research opportunities.

The emphasis is on understanding, experimentation, and independent thinking — not simply producing a finished paper.

How Mentoring Works

Mentoring is typically organized as a sequence of individual online sessions, with project work completed between meetings.

  • Identify an AI area that matches the student's interests.
  • Review relevant research and narrow the project into a clear question.
  • Build the required programming and machine-learning foundations.
  • Implement models and run experiments.
  • Analyze results and refine the methodology.
  • Structure, write, and revise a research paper or technical report.
  • For suitable projects, explore relevant journals, competitions, or other research outlets.

The technical depth and project scope are adjusted to the student's current level. Advanced programming experience is not required for every project.
