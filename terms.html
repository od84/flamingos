9. Research & Publication Support

For students enrolled in the Full Research Paper Track, Paper Mentor AI will support the student in developing a complete research paper and, where the project reaches an appropriate standard, preparing it for submission to a suitable journal, competition, conference, or research venue.

Publication Support Guarantee: For eligible Full Research Paper Track students who complete the agreed project requirements and mentoring work, Paper Mentor AI guarantees continued guidance through the preparation of at least one suitable submission, including paper revision, formatting, and submission preparation.

Acceptance or publication by any journal, conference, competition, or external organization cannot be guaranteed because final decisions are made independently by those organizations.
