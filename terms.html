1. Introduction

These Terms & Conditions (“Terms”) govern participation in Paper Mentor AI programs. By enrolling, you agree to abide by the following policies.

2. Program Structure & Sessions

All sessions are delivered online and typically run for 60 minutes. Programs are customized to each student’s goals, background, and pace.

3. Payment & Billing

  • Standard hourly rate: $100/hour
  • 10% discount on 10+ hour packages
  • Payment may be made upfront or in installments

4. Refund Policy

  • 100% refund after Session 1 if the student chooses not to continue.
  • Pro-rated refunds available at any time for unused hours.
  • Rescheduling permitted with 24 hours notice.

5. Responsibilities

Students must attend sessions with stable internet and required materials.

Mentors will provide professional, high-quality instruction and guidance.

6. Academic Integrity

Mentors assist with learning and research but do not complete schoolwork or write papers for students. All work must reflect the student’s own authorship.

7. Privacy & Data

All personal information is confidential and will not be sold or shared with third parties.

8. Limitation of Liability

Paper Mentor AI is not responsible for academic outcomes, admissions results, or external technical issues (e.g., internet connection).
