Applications & Prerequisites

When are applications due?
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. We recommend applying early, as spots fill quickly.
When will I hear back about my application?
You will typically receive an update within 3–5 days of submitting your application.
What happens after I submit my application?
You will receive an email confirmation and, if accepted, next steps to secure your spot and begin onboarding.
Are there any prerequisites? Is it okay if I’ve never coded before?
Absolutely! No prior coding experience is required.

Our curriculum is beginner-friendly, and many students join without any programming background. Advanced students will be placed in more challenging tracks with mentors experienced in working with high-level students.
Are there math requirements?
Basic algebra is sufficient. We teach all AI and coding concepts from the ground up.
Can students outside the U.S. join the program?
Yes! We work with students from over 25+ countries worldwide. All sessions are delivered online.

Program Pricing

How much does the program cost?
Our programs vary in duration and format. Detailed pricing is provided during your consultation session.
Do you offer scholarships or financial aid?
Yes — we offer limited financial aid based on demonstrated need. Please indicate interest in your application or email us for details.

Program Details

How big are class or mentoring groups?
We focus on highly personalized attention. Our 1:1 programs are entirely individualized.
Who are the mentors?
All mentors are graduate students or researchers from top universities (Cornell, EPFL, Stanford, UPenn) and work professionally in AI at leading tech companies. They are experienced in guiding students through real research projects.
Is there homework?
Most work is completed during live sessions. Toward the end of the program, students may spend 2–4 hours preparing their final project presentation.

After the Program

Will students receive a certificate?
Yes — all students who complete a project receive a certificate of completion.
Are course materials available afterward?
Yes, students retain access to project materials and resources after the program ends.
Do you offer follow-up programs?
Yes — students can continue with advanced AI projects or specialized research mentoring.

Middle School Students

Can 8th graders or rising freshmen join?
Yes — if they already have some programming experience. Otherwise, we recommend starting with foundational material.
Do you offer a middle school program?
We occasionally offer beginner-friendly versions of our program for younger students. Contact us for availability.
