Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about applications, pricing, prerequisites, and refund policies.

Applications & Prerequisites

When are applications due?
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Early application is recommended.
When will I hear back?
Most applicants receive an update within 1-2 days.
Do I need coding experience?
No. Many students start with zero experience. Advanced students receive more challenging projects.
Are there math requirements?
All AI/math concepts are taught from the ground up.
Do you accept international students?
Yes — students from 25+ countries participate. All sessions are online.

Pricing & Tuition

How much does the program cost?
Our standard rate is $100 per hour. Students enrolling for 10+ hours receive a 10% discount.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. Paper Mentor AI offers one of the most flexible refund policies:
  • 100% refund after Session 1 if you choose not to continue.
  • Pro-rated refund anytime — unused hours are fully refunded.
  • No penalty for early program completion.
Are there discounts available?
Yes — 10% off for 10+ hours. Additional support available for long-term research plans.

Program Details

How big are groups?
1:1 mentoring is fully individualized. Cohorts contain 2–3 students only.
Who are the mentors?
Mentors come from Cornell, EPFL, Stanford, UPenn, and top AI industry roles.
Is there homework?
Most work happens during sessions. Students may spend 2–4 hours on final project preparation.

After the Program

Do students receive a certificate?
Yes — all students completing a project receive a certificate.
Do students keep the materials?
Yes — all code, slides, and datasets remain available after completion.
Are follow-up programs available?
Yes — advanced AI, competitions preparation, and publication-focused mentoring.
