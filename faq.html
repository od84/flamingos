Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about applications, pricing, prerequisites, and refund policies.

Applications & Prerequisites

When are applications due?
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Early application is recommended to secure your preferred mentoring schedule.
When will I hear back?
Most applicants receive an update within 1–2 days with next steps and, if appropriate, a suggested mentor match.
Do I need coding experience?
No. Many students start with zero experience. Beginners cover the fundamentals first, while advanced students work on more challenging projects.
Are there math requirements?
All AI and math concepts are introduced from the ground up. We adapt the depth of theory to the student’s comfort level.
Do you accept international students?
Yes — students from multiple countries and timezones participate. All sessions are held online via video call.

Pricing & Tuition

How much does the program cost?
Our standard rate is $100 per hour. Students enrolling for 10+ hours receive a 10% discount.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. Paper Mentor AI offers a flexible refund policy:
  • 100% refund after Session 1 if you choose not to continue.
  • Pro-rated refund anytime — unused hours are fully refunded.
  • No penalty for early program completion.
Are there discounts available?
Yes — 10% off for 10+ hours. Additional support can be arranged for long-term research and publication-focused plans.

Program Details

How big are groups?
1:1 mentoring is fully individualized. When small cohorts are used, they typically contain 2–3 students only.
Who are the mentors?
Mentors are graduate-level researchers and practitioners in AI, with experience in both university research and industry applications.
Is there homework?
Most work happens during sessions, but students typically spend 2–4 hours on project preparation, experiments, or writing between meetings.

After the Program

Do students receive a certificate?
Yes — all students completing a project receive a certificate summarising their work and skills developed.
Do students keep the materials?
Yes — all code, slides, and datasets remain available after completion (subject to any third-party data licenses).
Are follow-up programs available?
Yes — advanced AI tracks, competition preparation, and publication-focused mentoring are available for returning students.
