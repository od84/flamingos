AI Research & Paper Mentoring for Ambitious Students

Paper Mentor AI pairs motivated high-school students with experienced AI researchers and graduate mentors to design, build, and publish high-level AI research. Our students frequently publish in competitive journals— including Stanford Intersect, Journal of Emerging Investigators (JEI), NHSJS, IJHSR, STEM Fellowship Journal, AJSR, and JHSS. We also provide strong recommendation letters.

All research is guided 1:1 by mentors from top universities such as Cornell, Columbia, and Stanford.

Where Our Mentors Come From

Our mentors are graduate students, researchers, and practitioners with strong AI and STEM backgrounds.

Many mentors are current or former graduate students and researchers at Cornell University and Stanford University.

Why Students Work with Paper Mentor AI

We focus on one outcome: helping students create real, technically rigorous AI research and polished papers.

  • 1:1 mentorship tailored to each student.
  • Mentors are AI-focused graduate students and researchers.
  • Students implement real models and experiments.
  • Full support for writing a publishable research paper.
  • Guidance on selecting journals and competitions.
