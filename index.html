AI Research & Paper Mentoring for Ambitious Students

Paper Mentor AI pairs motivated high-school students with experienced AI researchers and graduate mentors to design, build, and publish sophisticated AI research. Many students successfully publish in selective journals such as the Stanford Intersect, Journal of Emerging Investigators (JEI), National High School Journal of Science (NHSJS), International Journal of High School Research (IJHSR), STEM Fellowship Journal, American Journal of Student Research (AJSR), and the Journal of High School Science (JHSS). These publications help students stand out in competitive STEM programs and top university admissions.
