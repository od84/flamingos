Why Students Work with Paper Mentor AI

We focus on one outcome: helping students create real AI research and papers that genuinely matter — not toy examples or copied tutorials.

  • 1:1 mentorship tailored to each student’s background, interests, and goals.
  • Mentors are graduate students and practitioners in AI with strong research and industry experience.
  • Project-based learning: students implement real models, not just follow step-by-step instructions.
  • Structured support for research design, experiments, writing, and paper preparation.
  • A compelling research portfolio that supports applications to competitive universities and summer programs.

Examples of Student Research Topics

Project themes are adapted to each student’s interests and experience level while maintaining technical depth.

  • Detecting cyberbullying and hate speech in social media using transformer-based NLP models.
  • Brain tumor classification in MRI images with CNNs and explainable AI techniques.
  • Predicting stock market behaviour using LSTM networks and news sentiment.
  • Climate and weather anomaly forecasting with time-series machine learning.
  • Fake news and misinformation detection using natural language models.
  • Air quality and pollution prediction in major cities using historical sensor data.
