AI Research & Paper Mentoring for Ambitious Students

Paper Mentor AI pairs motivated high-school students with elite mentors from top universities such as Stanford, Cornell, Columbia, MIT, UC Berkeley, and more. Students work 1:1 with researchers and graduate mentors to design, build, and publish sophisticated AI research. Many students successfully publish in selective journals including Stanford Intersect, Journal of Emerging Investigators (JEI), NHSJS, IJHSR, STEM Fellowship Journal, AJSR, and JHSS.

For privacy reasons, full student papers are not publicly posted — however, publication samples can be shared upon request during consultation.
