Our Student Outcomes

Students in our mentoring program achieve meaningful academic milestones through guided research projects in AI, machine learning, and data science. Our focus is to help high-school students build impactful research portfolios that strengthen their university applications.

  • Students have published papers in peer-reviewed high-school research journals.
  • Many complete full research manuscripts that meet university-level expectations.
  • Students submit their work to national STEM competitions and research showcases.
  • Projects support successful applications to top U.S. and European universities.
  • Students gain experience normally acquired during the first years of university.

Real Student Research Examples

  • Cyberbullying detection using transformer-based NLP
  • Stock forecasting using LSTMs & sentiment analysis
  • MRI tumor classification with CNNs & Explainable AI
  • Sequence modeling for biological systems
  • Climate forecasting using deep learning
  • Satellite image classification for environmental monitoring
  • Energy consumption prediction using ML
  • Emotion recognition from speech using neural networks
  • Deepfake detection & image forgery identification
  • Recommendation systems for media & sports analytics

These projects have been used by students to strengthen applications to competitive global universities, including highly selective U.S. schools and top European technical institutions.

Why Publishing Matters

While publication is never required, many students choose to pursue it because:

  • It demonstrates academic maturity and initiative.
  • The peer-review process builds scientific and writing skills.
  • Published work gives students strong material for college essays, interviews, and recommendations.
  • It gives students a competitive edge for university applications and selective STEM programs.

Students are guided throughout the entire process — from research design to manuscript preparation — maximizing their chances of success.
