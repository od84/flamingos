Mentoring Programs

From your first line of Python to a full research paper, Paper Mentor AI offers flexible, high-impact mentoring tailored to your background and goals.

Program Structure

Each program is designed around a clear, practical outcome: a complete project or research paper that demonstrates real understanding and initiative.

  • Learn foundational Python, machine learning, and evaluation techniques.
  • Complete an applied AI project using real datasets and meaningful metrics.
  • Develop a full research paper suitable for student journals or STEM competitions (where appropriate).

1:1 Research Mentoring Tracks

Personalized and flexible mentoring for students who want meaningful research experience — not just another course.

Standard Research Track (≈ 8 hours) Mini Project
  • Python + ML fundamentals taught through a concrete mini-project.
  • Hands-on work in areas like computer vision, NLP, or time-series modelling.
  • Ideal for students new to AI who want a strong foundation and a finished project.
Advanced Research Track (≈ 12–16 hours) Research Paper
  • End-to-end research pipeline: from question design to modelling and evaluation.
  • Work with real-world datasets and modern deep learning tools.
  • Close guidance on scientific writing, structure, and visualisations.
