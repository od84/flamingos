Mentoring Programs

Whether you're just beginning with AI or aiming for a competitive research publication, Paper Mentor AI provides structured, high-impact mentoring tailored to every student.

Program Structure

All programs follow a progressive, research-focused structure that guides students from fundamentals to full project completion or publication-ready research work.

  • AI foundations, Python, and dataset exploration
  • Model development, analysis, tuning, and evaluation
  • Research writing, editing, and submission support

Session Plan

Session Breakdown (All Programs)
  • Sessions 1–2: Python basics, ML foundations, dataset overview
  • Sessions 3–5: Model development, evaluation, hyperparameter tuning
  • Sessions 6–7: Application of models, insights, error analysis
  • Final Sessions: Writing, editing, and preparing the final paper/report

1:1 Research Mentoring Tracks

Fully personalized mentoring designed to help students build real, meaningful research experience.

Standard Research Track (≈ 8 hours) Mini Project
  • Complete introduction to Python + ML fundamentals
  • Mini project with a real-world dataset
  • Model training and evaluation
  • Short research report (2–4 pages)
Advanced Research Track (≈ 12–16 hours) Research Paper
  • Full end-to-end research pipeline
  • Model comparison, tuning, and dataset preprocessing/augmentation
  • Advanced evaluation: cross-validation, interpretability, SHAP/LIME (optional)
  • Real-world dataset analysis and experimental insights
  • Full research paper (6–12 pages) with figures, methods, and results
  • Editing, revisions, proofreading, and support for journal or competition submission

Advanced Track — Session Plan

  • Session 1: Topic selection, dataset sourcing, research question formation
  • Session 2: Data cleaning, preprocessing, feature engineering
  • Sessions 3–4: Baseline model creation + evaluation
  • Sessions 5–6: Advanced models, tuning, augmentation, and ablation testing
  • Session 7: Results analysis, visualizations, interpretation
  • Sessions 8–10: Write full paper (Methods, Results, Introduction, Abstract)
  • Final Sessions: Revisions, proofreading, submission guidance
