Choose the Right Research Track

Students do not need to arrive with a finished research idea. The appropriate track depends on technical experience, available time, and the depth of research the student wants to pursue.

Research Foundations Track

Approximately 8 sessions

A structured introduction to applied AI research for students who want to learn the research process while completing a focused machine-learning project.

  • Python and machine-learning foundations where needed.
  • Selection of a focused research question.
  • Work with a real-world dataset.
  • Build and evaluate one or more ML models.
  • Interpret results and identify limitations.
  • Produce a short technical report or research-style write-up.
Best for: students who are new to AI research, want to build technical confidence, or would like to test whether a longer research project is right for them.

Full Research Paper Track

Typically 12–16+ sessions

A deeper research program designed for students who want to complete an end-to-end AI research project and develop a polished research paper.

  • Literature review and research-question development.
  • Dataset selection, cleaning, and exploratory analysis.
  • Baseline models and advanced model development.
  • Model comparison and systematic experimentation.
  • Error analysis and interpretation of results.
  • Research paper drafting and revision.
  • Guidance on identifying suitable publication, competition, or research outlets.
  • Submission preparation for suitable projects.
Best for: motivated students who want a substantial AI research experience and are prepared to work independently between mentoring sessions.

How a Full Research Project Develops

The exact timeline varies by student and project, but a complete research project typically moves through the following stages.

  • 1

    Research Question & Literature

    Identify an area of interest, review relevant prior research, and narrow the project into a focused, testable question.

  • 2

    Data & Methodology

    Select an appropriate dataset, define evaluation metrics, and design a clear experimental methodology.

  • 3

    Model Development

    Build baseline models and progressively test more advanced machine-learning approaches.

  • 4

    Experimentation & Evaluation

    Run experiments, compare models, investigate errors, and analyze whether the evidence supports the research hypothesis.

  • 5

    Research Writing

    Develop the introduction, methodology, results, discussion, figures, references, and conclusions into a coherent research paper.

  • 6

    Revision & Next Steps

    Revise the paper and, where appropriate, identify journals, competitions, or other suitable venues for submission.
