Mentoring Programs

From beginner-friendly introductions to full research papers, Paper Mentor AI offers flexible, high-impact mentoring tailored to each student’s background and goals.

Program Structure

Every program is designed around a clear, practical outcome: a complete project or research paper that demonstrates real understanding and initiative. Depending on the chosen track, students may:

  • Learn foundational Python, machine learning, and evaluation techniques.
  • Complete an applied AI project using real datasets.
  • Develop a full research paper suitable for journals or STEM competitions.

All pacing and project complexity are adjusted based on the student’s grade level, interests, and prior experience.

1:1 Research Mentoring Tracks

Personalized, flexible, and ideal for students who want to work on meaningful research at their own pace.

Standard Research Track (≈ 8 hours) Mini Project
  • Learn Python and core ML concepts with guided coding.
  • Complete a small research-style project using a curated dataset.
  • Understand how models work and how to interpret results.
  • Perfect for students with little or no AI background.
Advanced Research Track (≈ 12–16 hours) Full Research Paper
  • End-to-end research pipeline: problem definition, modeling, experimentation, and analysis.
  • Use real datasets from medicine, environment, finance, or custom domains.
  • Write a full research paper with mentor-guided revision.
  • Recommended for journal submissions or competitive STEM programs.

Small Cohort Options

Collaborative tracks with 2–3 students at a similar level.

AI Foundations Cohort
  • Python + ML fundamentals taught in small groups.
  • Live coding sessions with hands-on exercises.
  • Ends with a collaborative mini-project.
  • Best for beginners starting their AI journey.
Research Cohort Lab
  • Team-based research environment using a shared dataset.
  • Students collaborate on modeling, analysis, and writing.
  • Culminates in a group research presentation.
  • Ideal for students with some coding experience.

Pricing & Tuition

Simple, transparent pricing designed to fit different program lengths.

  • Standard Rate: $100 per hour
  • Discount: 10% off for 10+ hour packages
  • Flexible billing options available.

Refund Policy

  • 100% refund after Session 1: If the student chooses not to continue, the full amount is refunded immediately.
  • Pro-rated refund anytime: Families may request a refund for all unused hours at any stage in the program.
  • Sessions can be rescheduled with 24 hours notice.

Which Program Is Right for Me?

  • New to programming or AI: Foundations Cohort or Standard Track.
  • Some Python experience: Standard or Advanced Track.
  • Aiming for competitions or journals: Advanced Track or Research Cohort Lab.
