Our Mission

The primary goal of our program is to help high school students design, complete, and publish real AI research projects. These projects can be submitted to student research journals and to major U.S. STEM competitions such as:

  • Regeneron STS
  • ISEF (International Science & Engineering Fair)
  • JSHS (Junior Science & Humanities Symposium)
  • Local & national academic showcases

Completing a strong research project significantly strengthens university applications and demonstrates authentic interest in STEM.

1–1 Personalized Research Mentoring (8–12 Hours)

1–1 Standard Research Track (8 Hours)

  • Personalized AI mentorship tailored to the student
  • Learn ML + Python fundamentals
  • Start a research-grade project with guidance
  • Weekly 1–1 live sessions
  • Perfect for students new to AI or research

1–1 Advanced Research Track (12 Hours)

  • Full end-to-end research project
  • Model selection, training, experimentation & evaluation
  • Manuscript writing & research paper structure
  • Preparation for publication or competitions
  • Ideal for ambitious or experienced students

Small-Group Mentoring (3-Student Cohorts)

AI Foundations Group Program (3 Students)

  • Groups formed by similar level/experience
  • Interactive lessons & guided live coding
  • Collaborative mini research project
  • Total 8 hours of group mentoring

Group Research Lab (3 Students)

  • Team-based research environment
  • Learn advanced AI/ML topics together
  • Shared experimentation & model analysis
  • Perfect for students who enjoy teamwork

Which Program Is Right for You?

  • New to coding or AI? → Group Foundations or 1–1 Standard Track
  • Some experience with Python? → 1–1 Standard Track
  • Want to publish or compete? → Advanced Research Track
  • Prefer teamwork? → Group Research Lab
